# Small ad test plan: Project Evaluator

## What's settled
- Goal: free sign-ups that go on to run a first analysis
- Countries: Spanish-speaking Latin America, ads in Spanish
- Audience: everyone who evaluates business projects (entrepreneurs, SMEs, students, analysts, consultants)
- Budget: as close to free as possible. Nothing spends money without your OK on the exact amount.

## 1. Free first (no ad spend)
- Share the site in Spanish-speaking entrepreneur and finance communities, using tagged links so those visits show up in your stats.
- Make sure Google indexing is healthy (Search Console is already connected).

## 2. Very small paid test (only after you approve)
- Channel: Google Search, so the ads only show to people already searching for things like "calcular VAN y TIR", "evaluar proyecto de inversión" or "calculadora ROI".
- Suggested budget: USD 3/day (about USD 90/month). Minimum option: USD 2/day (about USD 60/month). Run it for 2 weeks, then review.
- The ads send people to the Spanish page (/es). There they can see the tool, sign up for free and run an analysis.
- The campaign is created **paused**. It only starts after you approve the exact amount.

## 3. Before it can run
- Connect or create a Google Ads account (an approval card will appear).
- Add sign-up tracking so Google can tell when someone signs up from an ad.
- Write the ad copy and keywords, and show them to you for approval.
- Check that the path from ad to sign-up to analysis works on your live site.
- Publish the version with the tracking.

## 4. After 2 weeks
- Review clicks, sign-ups and cost per sign-up. Then decide whether to keep it, adjust it or stop it.

## Technical details
- Search campaign through the Google Ads tool, with conversion goal = sign-up (Google tag event fired after a successful sign-up/OAuth callback).
- Keyword ideas scoped to Latin American markets, in Spanish. Cookie consent is handled before the tag goes in.
